Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love your gear. Here's how returns work.
01
Request a return
Contact us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and reason for return.
02
Pack it up
Repack the item in its original packaging, unused and with tags attached where applicable.
03
Ship it back
We'll email a return shipping label. Drop the package at any partner courier location.
04
Get refunded
Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll process your refund within 5–7 business days.

Return Window

You may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund to your original payment method. Items must be unused, in their original condition, and in the original packaging.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Resistance bands and fascia balls that have been used or removed from hygiene packaging
  • Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout
  • Gift cards

Exchanges

Need a different size or color? The fastest way is to return your original item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want. This ensures you get the replacement as quickly as possible.

Damaged or Defective Items

If your order arrives damaged or defective, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the item and packaging. We'll arrange a free replacement or full refund, including return shipping costs.

Return Shipping Costs

Returns due to a change of mind are subject to a flat return shipping fee, deducted from your refund. Returns due to our error (wrong item, damaged in transit, or defective) are always free.

Refund Timing

Once your return is received and inspected, refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Depending on your bank, it may take a few additional days to appear on your statement.

Need Help?

Reach out any time through our Contact page and we'll walk you through the process.
